with the columns `sensor_id,reading`.

   `answer.txt`.

   just its id to `highest.txt`.
3. Write `lookup.py` with two functions over all of `data/`:
   - `reading(sensor_id)` returns that sensor's reading as an `int`, or `None`
     for a sensor that is not there;
   - `region_max(region)` returns `(sensor_id, reading)` for the highest
     reading in that region, or `None` for a region that does not exist.
