- **Problem:** stripping earlier turns' thinking looks like a free prefill
  saving, but the model uses its own reasoning as working memory.
- **Eval case:** A12 against A3, rungs 1–5, 15 runs each, oMLX; link both
  records and say what A12 changed.
- **Evidence of the problem:** A3 15/15 at 54 s; A12 14/15 at 87 s. Prefill
  fell 57% (308k to 133k characters per run), output tokens rose 73% (5,344
  to 9,270), nudges rose from 2 to 6 and one final was still empty. Use the
  records' numbers; do not round a second time or add a derived rate.
- **Solution:** keep `reasoning_content` in the history. The current worker
  leaves it in place; show the smallest excerpt from
  `extensions/mellum-worker.ts` lines 20–25, not a new implementation. Link
  to the file.
- **Validation:** a before/after table for A3 and A12 with passes, prefill,
  output tokens, wall, nudges and empty finals. State that this is oMLX and
  the n is the record's 15 runs.
- **Interpretation:** name `cited` as the weakest badge; explain that oMLX's
  86% cache-hit prefill is why the saving is illusory; say the llama.cpp
  re-run is open. No new numbers.
