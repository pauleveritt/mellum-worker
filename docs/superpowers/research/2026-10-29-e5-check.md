# E5's lesson check: the challenge did not provoke the failure

**What is measured.** The preregistered E5 check
([prereg](2026-10-29-e5-check-prereg.md),
[record](../../ladder/2026-10-29-running-commands-check/README.md)), n = 3 per arm on the
vLLM host: before 3/3 passed, after 3/3 passed. No before session tried a
foreground server; one after session did (`uvicorn app:app --port 8000`), the
guard blocked it, and the model restarted it with `nohup … &` and passed.

**What is conjecture.** Why this challenge provokes the stall less often than
the AgentClinic suites did (four of nine bare sessions stalled there the same
day): the task is small and its step 1 leaves little to verify by hand.

## Against the rule

- The lesson works: **no** (before passed 3 of 3, not 0).
- The pathology is confirmed on this host by this challenge: **no** (0 of 3
  before sessions stalled). It was seen, once in six sessions, in the after
  arm, where the guard did what the lesson says it does.

## Decision

E5's page states this result and keeps saying its lesson check has not shown
FAIL → PASS. The challenge is revised before any further run, and the
revision is preregistered again; nothing was tuned between these runs. The
direction for the revision is the condition under which the stall was
recorded: a larger app whose check the model is inclined to make by running
it, as in the AgentClinic suites.

## What would falsify this

A revised challenge on which before sessions stall at least two times in
three: then the original challenge was too easy, not the pathology too rare.

## Risks

n = 3 per arm; the one guard block is a single observation.

## Revision 2 (2026-10-29)

Preregistered ([prereg](2026-10-29-e5-check-v2-prereg.md)): step 2 required a
live server (`health.txt` from `curl`). Before 3/3, after 3/3
([record](../../ladder/2026-10-29-running-commands-check/README.md)). Every
before session started a server and none hung: one backgrounded it with `&`
and the others got a response before moving on. In the after arm the guard
blocked six foreground starts across two sessions, and both went on to pass.

**Against the rule:** the lesson does not work on this challenge and the
pathology is not confirmed (0 of 3 before sessions stalled). The guard does
what the lesson says when the model starts a foreground server; on a short,
explicit task this model rarely does. The recorded stalls (four of nine bare
AgentClinic sessions) came from long sessions, so the next revision, if any,
is a long multi-phase task rather than a more insistent prompt.

**Two failures neither revision was looking for**, both in before sessions:
`killall -f uvicorn` (kills every uvicorn on the machine, a learner's own
servers included) and running tests through this repository's `.venv` by
absolute path, outside the workspace. Both are candidates for guards of
their own.
